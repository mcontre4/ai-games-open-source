# Third-party notices and assets

AI Games source is MIT licensed. Dependencies retain their own licenses; the root MIT license does not replace upstream terms. The lockfiles pin the exact dependency versions. Review dependencies and their notices when redistributing application binaries, containers or game packages.

The bundled Neon Comet ZIP includes the Phaser engine. Generated 3D/isometric packages include Three.js. Preserve the notices below when redistributing them. The Docker image also installs the Apache-2.0-licensed [OpenAI Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex); its distribution has its own license and notices.

The creature avatars and game-view selector illustrations are original AI-assisted project assets; Neon Comet art is generated by the project’s SVG rasterization script. These assets are distributed under the repository license to the extent the contributors hold rights. Avatar and selector prompts are recorded beside their assets in `generation.json`; sample art generation is in `scripts/create-art.ts`. The selector illustrations are illustrative examples, not evidence of playable generated games. The screenshots in `docs/images` are captures of this application's real interface and deterministic runtime fixtures, using synthetic accounts. No external stock photo library is used.
